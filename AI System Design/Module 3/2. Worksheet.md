# Model Selection Decision Memo

**Time:** 3–4 hours  
**Format:** Written memo (~2 pages)

---

## The Task

Choose one of the three scenarios below. Produce a model selection decision memo addressed to the technology leadership team. The memo should be specific enough that someone could implement the recommendation without needing a follow-up meeting. Do not read the model answer before completing your own.

---

## Scenario Options

### Scenario A — HealthBridge: Clinical Documentation AI

HealthBridge (regional hospital network, 8 hospitals, 40 clinics) wants to deploy an AI system that listens to physician-patient encounters, understands the clinical context, and auto-drafts clinical notes in SOAP format for physician review before they are written to the EHR (Epic). The system processes encounter audio transcripts and generates structured clinical documentation.

Key constraints: all patient data is PHI — subject to HIPAA; data cannot leave the HealthBridge private cloud. Physicians are time-pressured; latency matters. Clinical notes are legal documents — accuracy is non-negotiable. HealthBridge has a cloud infrastructure team but no ML engineering capability. Launch scale: 2,000 encounters per day. Projected scale at 18 months: 10,000 per day.

The model must handle long-form medical conversation transcripts (15–45 minutes of speech-to-text output), produce consistently structured SOAP-format notes, use correct medical terminology, and be evaluatable against physician-authored notes.

### Scenario B — LexAI: Legal Research and Drafting

Meridian & Partners (200-attorney law firm) wants an AI system for legal research and drafting. The system queries 10 years of internal case files and assists with drafting contracts, motions, and correspondence. Two distinct sub-tasks: (1) research synthesis — querying the knowledge base and producing a structured memo; (2) drafting — generating first-draft legal documents from matter context.

Key constraints: attorney-client privilege applies — client matter data cannot transit to any third-party system without explicit client consent. The IT team has 3 people; operational complexity must be minimal. Some partners are deeply skeptical; the system must demonstrably save time to earn adoption. Scale: 200 attorneys, roughly 50,000 document searches and 5,000 drafting tasks per month.

### Scenario C — RetailMind: Conversational Search and Shopping Assistant

ShopSphere (e-commerce, 15M registered users, 2M DAU, 8M SKUs) wants two AI capabilities: (1) conversational search — parsing natural-language queries into structured filters and a refined search query; (2) a conversational shopping assistant for higher-consideration purchases (guided product discovery over multiple turns). Both capabilities are user-facing and latency-sensitive.

Key constraints: GDPR applies to EU users — their data cannot leave EU infrastructure. The engineering team is strong (50+ engineers) but has no prior AI system experience. The recommendation surface currently drives 35% of purchases; any degradation in quality directly impacts revenue. Scale at launch: 2M DAU. Peak (Black Friday): 5× normal load.
