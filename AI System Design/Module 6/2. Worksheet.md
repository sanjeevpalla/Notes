# Evaluation Architecture Design

**Time:** 3–4 hours  
**Format:** Written plan (~3 pages)

---

## The Task

Choose one of the three scenarios below. Design the evaluation architecture for the AI system described — covering all three measurement layers, the golden dataset, online evaluation, business outcome tracking, and drift detection. Do not read the model answer before completing your own.

---

## Scenario Options

### Scenario A — HealthBridge: Clinical Documentation AI

HealthBridge's clinical documentation AI has been in production for three months. It generates SOAP-format note drafts from encounter transcripts. Physicians review and approve each draft. The system processes 2,000 encounters per day.

Current measurement state: infrastructure monitoring is in place (latency, error rate, token counts). That is all. Nobody knows whether the notes are clinically accurate. Nobody knows whether physicians are rubber-stamping or genuinely reviewing. Nobody knows if note quality has drifted since launch. The compliance team is asking what evidence exists that the system is performing as intended — and there is no answer. You have been asked to design the evaluation architecture that should have been in place from day one.

### Scenario B — LexAI: Legal Research and Drafting AI

Meridian & Partners' LexAI system has been live for four months. Attorneys use it for research queries and first-draft documents. The managing partner is asking a simple question: "Is this making attorneys faster, and is the output good enough to rely on?" Nobody can answer either part of that question with data.

Current measurement state: the system logs latency and error rates. There is an in-product thumbs-up/thumbs-down feedback widget that 8% of users ever click. That is all. You have been asked to design a complete evaluation architecture that can answer the managing partner's question — and detect when the answer changes.

### Scenario C — RetailMind: Conversational Search and Shopping Assistant

ShopSphere's conversational search and shopping assistant have been live for two months and are serving 2 million daily active users. Product leadership is being asked whether the AI investment is paying off. The answer is currently "the features seem to be working" — which is not an answer.

Current measurement state: infrastructure metrics are healthy. The shopping assistant has a post-conversation rating widget (5-star). Conversational search has no feedback mechanism. Neither feature has a business outcome metric linked to AI activity specifically. You have been asked to build the measurement architecture that proves — or disproves — that the AI investment is generating return.
