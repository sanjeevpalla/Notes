# Production Architecture Design

**Time:** 4–5 hours  
**Format:** Architecture diagram + written document (~3 pages)

---

## The Task

Choose one of the three scenarios below. Design the production architecture for the AI system described — covering scaling, cost, LLMOps, and production readiness. This is the final engineering design before the system goes to scale. Do not read the model answer before completing your own.

---

## Scenario Options

### Scenario A — HealthBridge: Clinical Documentation AI at Scale

HealthBridge's clinical documentation AI (designed across Modules 2–6) has completed its pilot with 500 physicians across two hospitals. The pilot result: physicians are satisfied, note accuracy is acceptable, and the compliance team has signed off on governance. The board has approved expansion to all 8 hospitals and 40 clinics.

Scale change: 2,000 encounters per day (pilot) → 10,000 encounters per day (full network) within 12 months, with a peak of 15,000 encounters per day during flu season. The system is self-hosted (HIPAA requirement). The infrastructure team has managed the pilot on a single GPU instance; they have no autoscaling experience and no runbook for AI-specific incidents. You have been asked to design the production architecture for the expansion.

### Scenario B — LexAI: Legal AI at Full Firm Scale

Meridian & Partners' LexAI system has been approved for rollout from the pilot group (40 attorneys) to all 200 attorneys across three offices. The pilot was run on a single self-hosted instance with no autoscaling, no prompt versioning system, and no formal deployment process — prompts were edited directly in the production configuration.

Scale change: 40 attorneys → 200 attorneys; 10,000 research queries/month → 50,000 research queries/month; 1,000 drafting tasks/month → 5,000 drafting tasks/month. One of the pilot attorneys used a poorly-worded prompt edit to break the system's citation format for three days before anyone noticed. The managing partner has made it clear this cannot happen again at full scale. You have been asked to design the production architecture and LLMOps operating model for the full rollout.

### Scenario C — RetailMind: E-Commerce AI at Peak Scale

ShopSphere's conversational search and shopping assistant have been live for three months at baseline load (2M DAU). Black Friday is 10 weeks away. The system has never been tested at peak load. Engineering knows from the existing Elasticsearch infrastructure that Black Friday brings 5–8× normal traffic for 72 hours. The AI components have no autoscaling configuration beyond the defaults. The cost model has not been validated at peak. You have been asked to design the production architecture that will survive Black Friday and the holiday season beyond it.
